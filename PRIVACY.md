# Local Cookie Sync — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-07_

Local Cookie Sync is a developer tool. It copies browser cookies from a website you choose to another
address you choose (typically a local development server) within your own browser.

## What the extension accesses

- **Cookies** on sites you have granted it access to. Besides `localhost` and `127.0.0.1`, the
  extension can only access a site after you approve Chrome's permission prompt for it. Cookies are
  read only when you click a button in the popup.
- **The address of the current tab**, used only to pre-fill the source site in the popup.
- **Settings** (the source and target addresses you last used), saved in Chrome's local extension
  storage on your computer.

## What it does with that data

Cookies are read from the source site and written to the target address you choose, within your own
browser. If you click **Copy as script**, they are placed on your clipboard.

The extension does **not** send any data to the developer or to any other server. It has no
analytics, does not track you, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

## Removing data

Uninstalling the extension deletes its saved settings and site access. You can also revoke access to
individual sites from the extension's details page in `chrome://extensions`. Cookies copied to a
target can be cleared from Chrome's site settings for that address.
